Tips for accurate results

  • Shoot straight from above, with the ruler lying flat in the same plane as the object.
  • Use a plain, contrasting background and even lighting (avoid strong shadows).
  • Always check the ruler handles – zoom in with the magnifier and place them exactly on two tick marks, then type their distance.
  • If the outline is wrong, drag a box around the object or tap on it, and adjust the sensitivity.

Chrome AI uses Gemini Nano running locally in Chrome (Prompt API with image input). Your photo never leaves your device. If it is not available, everything still works with the built-in image analysis and manual adjustment.
